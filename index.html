Research Topics

Next-generation Electrochemical Cells (SOFC, SOEC, PCEC)

Development and evaluation of Solid Oxide Fuel Cells (SOFC) and Electrolysis Cells (SOEC) for highly efficient energy conversion and water splitting. Current focus includes Protonic Ceramic Electrochemical Cells (PCEC) for low-temperature operation.

Degradation Mechanisms & Material Characterization

Investigating core degradation mechanisms in electrochemical systems, such as Strontium (Sr) segregation under operational stress. Utilizing advanced characterization tools (TEM, SEM) and computational calculations (DFT) to modify electrodes for enhanced durability.
